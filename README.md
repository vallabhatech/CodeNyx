#  CodeNyx

A simple app that runs your entire hackathon in one place.

---

##  What it does

CodeNyx helps participants:

- Join their team  
- Stay updated with announcements  
- Check the schedule  
- Share progress on a live feed  
- Ask mentors for help  
- Submit their final project  

No confusion. No chaos. Just everything in one app.

---

##  Why it exists

Hackathons usually feel messy — too many groups, missed updates, no coordination.

CodeNyx fixes that by becoming the **single source of truth** for the entire event.

---

## 📱 Key Features

- 👥 Team-based login  
- 📢 Live announcements  
- 🗓️ Event schedule  
- 🌍 Social feed (posts + images)  
- 🧑‍🏫 Mentor requests  
- 📦 One-click project submission  

---

##  Goal

Make hackathons feel smooth, organized, and actually enjoyable.
